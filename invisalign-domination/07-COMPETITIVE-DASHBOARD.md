# 4. THE WINNING FORMULA

## What ALL High-Performing Diamond Apex Practices Have in Common That We Currently Do Not

Cross-referencing Parrock, Smile Cliniq, Banning, Ruh, and EvoDental reveals eight structural patterns that every high-performer shares and we currently lack. These are not individual tactics — they are the formula.

---

### Pattern 1: A Named, Valued Free Diagnostic as the Consultation Hook

**Evidence:**
- Parrock: "Free CT Scan — value £250/jaw, £500 full mouth — when treatment booked at consultation"
- Banning: "Free 3D scan + digital X-rays + ClinCheck preview — all at no cost"
- EvoDental: "Free 90-minute consultation including CBCT scan and same-day suitability decision"
- Oradent Ashford (local competitor): "Free consultation worth £50 + free whitening worth £295"
- Ruh Dental: "Free 3D digital scan + simulation — worth £300" (Open Week)

**Our position:** "Free consultation" — no scan hook, no stated value, no tangible deliverable.

**The pattern:** Every market leader offers something with a price attached. The word "free" alone is no longer a conversion driver — the market has trained patients to expect it. The conversion driver is a specific diagnostic that has a real-world value the patient can verify (e.g., "a CBCT scan costs £300 privately — yours is included"). This also pre-qualifies the patient's case and gives the clinician a clinical reason to reach a decision at the consultation.

**Required action:** Every GM Dental consultation CTA must become "Free 3D Smile Scan + Implant/Invisalign Assessment — value £350 — at no cost to you." The scan must be named. The value must be stated. The outcome (you will see your result before you start) must be described.

### Pattern 2: Daily or Monthly Cost Anchor for Every High-Ticket Treatment

**Evidence:**
- Parrock: "From £1.70 per day" for All-on-4 (the lead headline in their PPC ad for same-day teeth)
- Banning: "From £15.74 per month" for Invisalign (primary price display on Invisalign page)
- Anele de Novo (Herne Bay, direct rival): "From £7.50/day" for dental implants
- EvoDental: "From £179/month" for full arch (stated example: deposit £950, loan £8,550 over 60 months)

- Romney House (confirmed landing page): Invisalign from £2,995 — with finance to spread the cost

**Our position:** Lump-sum pricing only (where pricing is shown at all).

**The pattern:** No patient mentally approves a £9,500 expenditure. Every patient approves a £179/month expenditure. The daily or monthly anchor reframes the decision from "can I afford this" to "this costs less than my Netflix subscription." The lump sum is still disclosed (regulatorily required) but it is presented after the monthly frame, not before it.

**Required action:** Every treatment over £1,000 on every GM Dental page must display the monthly payment equivalent directly adjacent to the total price. "Invisalign from £2,800 — or from £X per month with 0% finance." "Full arch implants from £X — or from £X per day over 60 months." Implement this on every page, every ad, every landing page within Week 1.

**The critical follow-up:** If a patient does not accept within 7 days, the TC calls (not emails) with: "I just wanted to check if you had any questions about your plan — and I wanted to let you know we have a space on [specific date] if you would like to get started." Specific date and time creates decision urgency without artificial pressure.

**Tool needed:** Finance partner integration into CRM. TC follow-up task automatically created in CRM 7 days post-consultation for all non-converted consultations.

**Competitor benchmark:** Banning's entire proposition is built on making the monthly payment feel accessible at the decision moment. Smile Cliniq has an online 0% Finance Calculator on site — an interactive tool that converts price anxiety into a monthly payment figure. The goal is to remove the gap between "I want this" and "I can afford this" at the same moment.

**Metric:** Finance acceptance rate as a proportion of patients who applied: target 75%+ approval (subject to credit eligibility of patient cohort). Time from consultation to finance acceptance target: under 14 days.

---

### STEP 7: TREATMENT START — THE HANDOFF TO CLINICAL TEAM

**What happens:** Patient pays deposit (or finance is activated), signs consent, and books their first clinical appointment. The journey moves from marketing to clinical.

**Marketing actions at this step:**
1. Welcome email confirming first appointment, what to expect, who they will see.
2. SMS reminder 48 hours before appointment.
3. Pre-appointment patient portal access (if available) for medical history forms and treatment consent.
4. The TC assigns the patient a "case tracker" or equivalent — a named point of contact through the treatment journey who is not the clinician but can answer non-clinical questions.

**Tool needed:** Dental PMS with automated appointment reminders. Patient portal (Dentally, Carestream, or equivalent). Pre-appointment form capability.

**Metric:** Treatment start (deposit paid) vs finance accepted rate target: 90%+ — patients who accept finance should start treatment. Any gap here indicates a clinical or scheduling friction point that must be diagnosed.

---

### STEP 8: POST-TREATMENT — REVIEW CAPTURE, REFERRAL, AND UPSELL

**What happens:** Treatment completes. The patient has the result. The marketing job now is: capture the review, generate the referral, and identify the next treatment opportunity.

**Review capture:** Within 4 hours of the final appointment (or following an agreed milestone mid-treatment), automated SMS: "Hi [Name], we hope you are delighted with your smile progress! Could you spare 2 minutes to share your experience on Google? Your review helps other [Ashford / Herne Bay] patients find us. [Direct Google Review Link]." This one SMS, at the right moment, with the right direct link, is worth more to the practice than any advertising investment in driving new review velocity.

**Referral mechanism:** "Share your result and earn £[X] toward any future treatment." Or: "Refer a friend and you both receive free whitening." The referral CTA must be in the review-capture SMS and in the post-treatment email. Ruh Dental's Molly-Mae moment proved that peer sharing is more efficient than paid advertising for the cosmetic patient cohort.

**Upsell pathway:** Invisalign complete → whitening (if not bundled) → composite bonding → annual retainer check. Implant complete → implant aftercare plan → annual hygienist visits → adjacent teeth cosmetics. A patient who has invested £3,000–£9,000 in their smile has shown willingness to invest in dental aesthetics. They are the warmest possible lead for adjacent treatments.

**Competitor benchmark:** EvoDental has a formal dentist referral programme (B2B) alongside consumer referrals. Parrock promotes their membership plan (£45/month) to post-treatment patients. Banning's "Smile Club" membership plan retains patients at low monthly cost and provides hygiene income between cosmetic cases. GM Dental needs a membership plan offer presented at the post-treatment appointment.

**Metric:** Post-treatment review capture rate target: 40%+ of all completing patients leave a Google review within 7 days (with SMS prompt — industry benchmark is 30–45% response rate on direct-link SMS review requests). Referral rate target: 15%+ of Invisalign completions generate at least one named referral enquiry within 90 days.
