## THE FINANCE MATHS (so the "per month" / "per day" numbers are real)

The `from £X/month` figures above assume **0% APR** over a standard term. Change the term to match
what your finance provider (e.g. Tabeo, Chrysalis, Medenta) actually offers, and the monthly figure
recalculates. Formula: **monthly = total ÷ number of months**.

| Treatment total | ÷ 12 mo | ÷ 24 mo | ÷ 36 mo | ÷ 48 mo | ÷ 60 mo |
|-----------------|--------:|--------:|--------:|--------:|--------:|
| £2,500 (Invisalign Lite) | £208 | £104 | **£69** | £52 | £42 |
| £3,900 (Invisalign Comp.) | £325 | £163 | £108 | **£81** | £65 |
| £2,400 (Single implant) | £200 | £100 | **£66** | £50 | £40 |
| £5,500 (Multiple implants) | £458 | £229 | £153 | £115 | £92 |
| £12,995 (All-on-4 / arch) | — | £541 | £361 | £271 | **£217** |

**"From £X per day" framing** (Parrock uses "£1.70/day" for All-on-4): daily = monthly ÷ 30.
- £12,995 over 60 months = £217/mo = **£7.13/day**
- To advertise "£1.70/day" you need a ~£15,000 total spread over ~24 years, OR a deposit + long term —
  that number is a *finance-provider marketing construct*, not the real cash price. **Confirm what your
  provider will actually underwrite before using a per-day figure in an ad.**
