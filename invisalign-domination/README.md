## The bottom line
- **Nobody owns local search authority** (top rival DR 39; many famous names DR <30) — local SEO in Kent + North London is genuinely winnable.
- **30%+ net margin is achievable** on implants now; on Invisalign it requires Diamond/Apex volume + controlled delivery cost + held premium fee + lean blended CPA (£150–250) + diluted overhead.
- **Parrock Dental is the benchmark** (Diamond Apex, 8,000+ implants, 826 reviews, 8,340 IG followers) — this is the target. Parrock has radio ads (Smooth Kent + Heart Kent), 28 years of brand equity, and 30 years in practice. The gap closes through review velocity, content output, and paid ads in the next 24 months.
- **Your biggest single lever in Ashford:** Aligndent Ashford (Diamond Apex) has only 29 Google reviews and 135 Instagram followers. A funded, content-active competitor with free consultations wins this market.
- **Warwick Lodge / Herne Bay is the implant hub:** RCS-trained implant dentist on site. Catchment 37k local + 150k within 15 miles. EvoDental now has a Dartford clinic — but they do full-arch only. Single and multiple implants in Herne Bay are wide open.
- **Group tier aggregation:** If Align Technology recognises your 5-practice group as a single entity, 400+ cases/year across the group qualifies for Diamond Apex status group-wide. Verify with your Align rep immediately — this is worth £150k–£200k/year in lab fee savings.
