### 3.5 The Review Engine — Non-Negotiable

Parrock has 826+ Google reviews. Smile Cliniq claims 800+. You are at **161 reviews for Warwick Lodge** and likely lower for the other practices.

**The review system:**

1. **Timing:** Ask for a review at the happiest moment — when the patient sees their final result (Invisalign removal + scan reveal, implant crown fit)
2. **Delivery method:** Send a direct SMS/WhatsApp with a pre-filled Google review link — single tap to open, one click to 5-star. If they open it, 80%+ will leave a review.
3. **Staff ownership:** The TCO asks every completing patient. Track per team member.
4. **Target:** 25–30 new Google reviews per location per month until hitting 300+. After 300, maintain 10–15/month.
5. **Respond to every review** within 48 hours. Use the response to include keywords naturally: "So glad you're happy with your Invisalign results in Herne Bay — it was a pleasure treating you."
6. **Never buy reviews.** One fake review investigation can trigger a Google knowledge panel suspension.

**Why this matters more than almost anything:** When a patient searches "Invisalign Herne Bay" on Google Maps, they see practices ranked by proximity + relevance + rating × volume. A practice with 400 reviews at 4.7★ outranks a competitor with 30 reviews at 5.0★ every time.
