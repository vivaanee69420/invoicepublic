# The Invisalign Domination Playbook — Kent & North London

> A channel-by-channel operating manual to take you to **Diamond → Diamond Apex** and a **30%+ net margin**. Pair this with the ROI calculator (set your own numbers) and the competitor teardown.

---

## 0. The strategy in one page

**Positioning:** Become the **#1 Invisalign destination in your specific towns** — not "a dentist that also does Invisalign," but *the* clear-aligner brand locally. Niche beats generalist (this is the MiSmile lesson).

**The growth equation:**
> **Cases = Reach × Lead-rate × Book-rate × Show-rate × Close-rate**, and **Profit = Cases × (Fee − COGS − Clinical − CPA − Admin − Overhead/case)**

You attack **every term** simultaneously:
1. **Reach** — own local SEO + Google Maps + always-on paid + organic social.
2. **Lead → Book → Show** — speed-to-lead, TCO follow-up, deposits/reminders.
3. **Close** — TCO consult + iTero simulation + 0% finance + same-day start.
4. **Margin** — climb the tier ladder (COGS ↓), control CPA (channel mix), systematise delivery.

**The margin truth (from the model):** an associate at a 45% split caps net margin near **25%**; the route to a true **30–35%+ implant-equivalent margin** is **Diamond/Apex scale + Apex COGS discount + lean blended CPA (£150–250) + premium fee held + a controlled/owner-leveraged, TCO-heavy delivery model.** Volume *is* the margin strategy.

## 1. Pricing & offer architecture

**Publish transparent, all-inclusive, finance-first pricing.** The fastest-scaling competitors (Ruh, Sensu, Smilepod) all do. Hide pricing and you lose the affordability-driven buyer.

**Recommended Kent / North London ladder (set against the £4.5k Comprehensive market median; North London can sit +10–20% over Kent):**

| Product | Headline | Lead with monthly |
|---|---|---|
| i7 / Express | from £1,800 | from £75/mo (0% × 24) |
| Lite | from £2,950 | from £123/mo |
| Comprehensive | from £3,900 (Kent) / £4,400 (N. London) | from £163/mo |
| Teen / First | from £3,200 | from £133/mo |
| **Retainers & whitening** | bundled "Smile for Life" add-on | protect & upsell |

**Offer mechanics that convert (use, don't abuse):**
- **Free consultation + free iTero 3D "see your new smile" scan** — the universal hook.
- **0% finance over 12–24 months, low/no deposit** — make the *monthly* number the hero in every ad.
- **Time-boxed incentive** rather than discounting the fee: "Book your free scan this month → free whitening + free retainers (worth £600)." Protect price integrity; add value instead of cutting.
- **Same-day start:** scan + ClinCheck + commit in one visit kills drop-off.

**Do not race Ruh to the bottom on price.** Their "lowest ever" promo is a high-volume London-specialist play. Your edge is *local dominance + premium experience + social proof*, sustaining a higher fee.

## 2. Local SEO — the winnable long game (your durable moat)

The DR data proves it: the local field is soft (most DR 24–39; your N. London rival Smile Cliniq DR 39 is the bar). SEO compounds and lowers blended CPA over time — it is how you get cases at near-zero marginal cost so the *blended* CPA hits £150.

**Per-location program (run for every site/town):**
1. **Google Business Profile (GBP) per location** — the highest-ROI local asset. Fully optimised: "Invisalign Provider" + dentist categories, services, products with prices, weekly posts, Q&A seeded, and a **review engine targeting Google specifically** (the apex players are weak here — most have no confirmed Google counts). Target **>150 Google reviews/location at 4.8★+**.
2. **Location landing pages** — one strong page per town you want to own: *"Invisalign in [Maidstone / Tunbridge Wells / Bromley / Finchley / Islington / Barnet]."* Each with local pricing, finance, before/afters from local patients, FAQs, embedded map, reviews.
3. **Service hub + supporting content** — `/invisalign/` pillar + clusters: cost, vs braces, vs cheap mail-order aligners, treatment timeline, teen, "Invisalign near me," before/after gallery.
4. **Technical & E-E-A-T** — fast mobile site, schema (LocalBusiness, Dentist, FAQ, Review, Product/Offer), named clinician bios with GDC numbers + Invisalign tier badges.
5. **Local links + citations** — consistent NAP across directories; local PR (sponsorships, schools, wedding fairs, gyms, local press); supplier/Invisalign locator listings.

**Targets:** rank top-3 for "invisalign [your towns]" and "invisalign near me" within the catchment in 6–9 months; out-rank Smile Cliniq (DR 39) in Finchley/St John's Wood with location-page depth + review velocity they don't have.

## 3. Google Ads — the fast tap for high-intent demand

Captures people *actively searching* — highest intent, best closers. Run alongside SEO (own the SERP twice).

- **Campaigns:** (a) Search — Invisalign brand/category terms by town; (b) Search — "near me"/cost/clear braces; (c) Performance Max or Local for Maps prominence; (d) competitor-brand campaign (bid on "Smile Cliniq / [local rivals]" — legal, effective); (e) remarketing.
- **Keywords:** `invisalign [town]`, `invisalign near me`, `invisalign cost [town]`, `clear braces [town]`, `teeth straightening [town]`, `invisalign finance/monthly`. Expect **CPC £8–£25** in your metros.
- **Landing pages:** dedicated, not the homepage. One per campaign/town. Above the fold: before/after, monthly price, free-scan CTA, finance, reviews, trust badges. **Target landing-page CVR 8–15%** (optimised) vs 2–5% default.
- **Tracking:** call tracking + form + WhatsApp; offline-conversion import so Google optimises to *started cases*, not just leads.
- **Budget logic:** at CPL £45–£120 and consult→start ~70%, model your CPA on the funnel sheet. Start **£3,000–£6,000/mo/location**, scale what converts.

## 4. Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Ads — manufacture demand + retarget

Meta *creates* demand among the 28–45 female core who weren't actively searching. Cheaper leads, lower intent → **TCO follow-up is everything.**

- **Objective:** Lead-gen (instant forms) + Conversions to landing page; always-on retargeting of site visitors and video-viewers.
- **Creative (this is 80% of Meta success):**
  - **Video before/after smile reveals** (the single best-performing asset — see §6). Real patients, real voices.
  - Patient testimonial clips (30–45s, talking-head, emotional benefit not features).
  - "Cost/finance" creative: "Straighten your teeth from £X/month, 0% finance."
  - Founder/clinician face-to-camera (personal brand sells — the Rhona Eskander/Smiles By Gurms lesson).
  - Offer creative: free scan + this-month value bundle.
- **Benchmarks:** CPM £8–£18; **CPL £25–£75**. Lower intent, so expect lower close than Google — *blend* both.
- **Audiences:** local radius (your catchment), 25–55, interests (weddings, beauty, fashion, fitness), lookalikes of past patients, full retargeting stack.

## 5. The "before & after" content system (your #1 conversion asset)

Every apex player runs on this. Build a **factory**, not occasional posts.

**Capture protocol (standardise across all sites):**
- Consistent **clinical photography** — same angles, lighting, retracted + smiling, day-1 vs final.
- **Video reveals** — film the reaction; short-form vertical for Reels/TikTok.
- **Consent & rights** at consult (model release covering ads + organic).
- **Tag the patient** (with consent) — extends organic reach (the Smiles By Gurms tactic).

**Output cadence (per week, per brand):**
- 3–5 short-form videos (Reels/TikTok): before/after reveals, patient stories, "day in the life," myth-busting, finance explainer.
- 2–3 feed posts (carousels of before/afters, reviews, team).
- Daily stories (behind-the-scenes, polls, Q&A, scan footage).
- Repurpose winners into **paid ads** (organic-proven creative converts best).

**Channels:** Instagram + TikTok primary (where the buyer is); YouTube for long-form authority; Google Business posts for local SEO. A named clinician "face" account *plus* the practice account.

## 6. The conversion funnel & TCO engine (where money is won or lost)

Leads are worthless without conversion. The winners run a **Treatment Coordinator** model.

**The journey:**
1. **Speed-to-lead:** contact every lead **within 5 minutes** (call + SMS + WhatsApp). This single factor can double booked-consults.
2. **Book the free scan**, not "an enquiry." Take a **small refundable deposit** to lift show-rate.
3. **Reminders:** SMS/email/WhatsApp sequence → reduce no-shows.
4. **TCO consult:** iTero "ClinCheck/Outcome Simulator" *wow* moment → present **monthly finance** → handle objections → **same-day start**. Benchmark **consult→start 64–68%; aim 75%+.**
5. **Nurture non-closers:** 6–12 month email/SMS sequence (most "no"s are "not now").
6. **Post-treatment:** retainers, whitening, reviews, referrals ("refer a friend → £X off retainers").

**KPIs to dashboard weekly (per location):** leads, cost/lead, % contacted <5min, consults booked, show-rate, consult→start %, CPA, cases started, blended fee, net contribution/case.

## 7. Ad-spend & CPA targets (what to actually budget)

Using the model's funnel math (CPL £60, book 55%, show 80%, close 70% → **paid CPA ≈ £195**; blended ≈ £120 at 60% paid share):

| Stage | Cases/mo target | Paid mix | Indicative spend/mo (group) | Marketing % of rev |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Now (Diamond push) | 20–35 | ~60% paid | £6k–£12k | ~8–10% |
| Scale (→Apex) | 35–60 | ~50% paid (SEO/referral rising) | £10k–£18k | ~7–9% |
| Dominate (Apex held) | 60+ | ~40% paid | £12k–£20k | ~6–8% |

**Rules:** (1) Hold **CPA below ~10–12% of fee** (≤£400–£500 worst case; aim £150–250 blended). (2) Shift mix toward SEO/referral over time to drop blended CPA — that's the margin lever. (3) Spend behind **proven creative** only; kill losers fast. (4) Track to **started cases**, never leads.

## 8. The path to a 30%+ net margin (implant-equivalent)

From the Solver (set your own numbers):

| Lever | Mid-tier today | Diamond | Apex-optimised |
|---|---|---|---|
| Blended fee | £3,400 | £3,800 | £4,100 |
| Tier discount on lab | 10% | 38% | 46% |
| Blended CPA | £400 | £250 | £170 |
| Associate split | 45% | 45% | 40% + bonus |
| Fixed overhead/case | £450 | £220 | £150 |
| **Net margin** | **~1–15%** | **~20–25%** | **~35%** |

**The five moves, in priority order:**
1. **Climb to Diamond/Apex** → biggest COGS cut + marketing co-funding (volume = margin).
2. **Control delivery cost** → the single biggest lever. Productivity-based associate pay (not flat 45%), TCO offload, owner-leverage on complex/high-value cases. *This is what separates 25% from 35%.*
3. **Hold premium fee** → brand + before/afters + reviews let you sit above the discounters.
4. **Drive blended CPA to £150–250** → grow SEO/referral share so paid is the minority of starts.
5. **Dilute fixed overhead** → more cases through the same rooms/reception/software.

## 9. 90-day quick-start

**Days 0–30 — Foundations**
- Publish transparent finance-first pricing on every site; build dedicated Invisalign + per-town landing pages.
- Optimise every Google Business Profile; launch the Google-review engine (target +20/location/mo).
- Stand up tracking (call tracking, GA4, offline conversions, CRM/lead dashboard).
- Launch the before/after capture protocol + consent at every consult.
- Hire/assign and script a **Treatment Coordinator**; implement <5-min speed-to-lead + deposit + reminders.

**Days 31–60 — Switch on demand**
- Launch Google Search (per town) + Meta lead-gen with before/after + finance creative.
- Ship 3–5 short-form videos/week; start retargeting.
- Weekly KPI review; A/B landing pages and ad creative.

**Days 61–90 — Optimise & scale**
- Double down on winning creative/keywords; cut losers.
- Begin local-SEO content cluster + local link/PR push.
- Tighten consult→start with TCO coaching; launch referral + reactivation sequences.
- Review tier progress vs Diamond threshold; renegotiate lab pricing as volume climbs.

## 11. Risk & compliance notes
- **GDC/ASA advertising rules:** before/after claims must be genuine, with consent; no misleading "guarantees"; finance promotions need FCA-compliant representative APR wording. Keep model-release + consent records.
- **Clinical governance:** scaling volume must not outrun clinical quality — refinement rates and complex-case selection protect both outcomes and margin.
- **Don't over-index on one channel** — diversify across SEO, Google, Meta, referral so no single CPA spike threatens the model.
