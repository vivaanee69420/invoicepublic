## 2. Cost of goods to the dentist (COGS) — *largely ESTIMATE*

- **Align global ASP ≈ US$1,335 per case (2023)** — **VERIFIED** (Align SEC/IR). This is the average price Align *receives* across all products and tiers — the only hard anchor.
- **UK per-case lab fee (ESTIMATE, FX-derived, pre-discount):** Comprehensive **£900–£1,300**; Lite/Go **£500–£800**; i7/Express **£300–£500**.
- **ClinCheck:** bundled into the case lab fee (no separate charge).
- **Refinements:** included in Comprehensive within the treatment window; capped on Lite/Go.
- **Vivera retainers to practice:** **£250–£500/arch** (sets of 4) — frequently re-charged to the patient.
- **Tier discount swing on lab fee:** ~10% (Silver) → ~38% (Diamond) → **~46% (Diamond II/Apex)** — US-VERIFIED, UK-ESTIMATE.

**Why this matters:** moving from Gold/Platinum to Diamond/Apex can cut your per-case COGS by **£250–£400**. On 400 cases/year that's **£100k–£160k of pure margin** unlocked by volume alone.

## 4. Clinical delivery / chair time (VERIFIED ranges)

| Appointment | Minutes |
|---|---|
| Consult / TCO assessment | 30–45 (often free, TCO-led) |
| Records / iTero scan + ClinCheck | 30–45 |
| Fit + attachment bonding | 30–45 |
| Reviews (every 8–12 weeks) | 15–30 each |
| Refinements | extra cycle |

Simple case ≈ 3–4 visits / 6 months; full case 6–10+ visits. **Total active chair time ≈ 3–5 hrs/case.**

**Associate economics:** UK private associate split **40–55% of gross (50% typical)**, London/SE at the higher end; lab fee usually deducted first and often shared 50/50. **This split is the single biggest determinant of net margin** (see ROI model). A **Treatment Coordinator (TCO)** model offloads consults to protect dentist chair time and lifts conversion.

Sources: ruhdental.com; guildfordorthodontics.com; elev8searchgroup.co.uk; bda.org.

**The CPA formula that matters** (don't trust a single quoted CPA):
> **CPA = Cost-per-lead ÷ (lead→book × show-rate × consult→start)**

At CPL £60, book 55%, show 80%, close 70% → **paid CPA ≈ £195/started case**. Blend with organic/referral (≈ zero marginal cost) at 60% paid share → **blended ≈ £117/case**. That is the number that makes the economics work.

Sources: wordstream.com; patientgain.com; orthia.io; dominatedental.com; whitehat-seo.co.uk (agency figures = directional).

## 6. Who buys Invisalign (demographics)

- **Adults ~74% of UK clear-aligner revenue (2024); female ~61%; core adult age 26–35.** UK treats **>200,000 cases/year.** Motivation is aesthetic-led — 62% reject visible appliances. (VERIFIED: grandviewresearch.com; parkorthodontics.co.uk; covedentist.uk.)
- **Kent affluence:** Sevenoaks & Tunbridge Wells most affluent — gross disposable household income **>£33,000/head (2021)** vs Kent average £22,978; home ownership TW 65%, Sevenoaks 71.5%. (VERIFIED: kent.gov.uk; ONS.)
- **North London incomes:** median salary **£40.0k (Enfield) → £53.2k (Islington)**; catchment populations Barnet 389k, Enfield 330k, Haringey 264k, Islington 217k, Camden 210k. (VERIFIED: plumplot.co.uk.)

**Targeting implication:** primary persona is a **28–45 year-old woman, household income £40k+, motivated by appearance and weddings/events/photos/career**, who buys on **monthly affordability** and **social proof (before/afters + reviews)**. Secondary: teens (mum is the buyer) and professional men 30–50.

## 7. Market size & growth

- **UK clear-aligner market ≈ $290.6M (Grand View) / $325M (MRF), 2024; CAGR ~17% (MRF) to ~30% (GVR).** (VERIFIED-vendor; methodologies differ ~2×.)
- **Align FY24:** revenue ~$4.0bn (+3.5%); 2.5M cases (+3.5%); Q4 volume +6.1% YoY with EMEA/teen strength; management calls the market "vastly underpenetrated." (VERIFIED: Align IR.)

**Takeaway:** a double-digit-growth category that is still early — share is there to be taken by whoever markets and systematises best, not whoever has been around longest.
