## HOW TO USE THESE ASSETS

### Step 1 — Edit in Canva (5 minutes each)
1. Click the edit link for the recommended design
2. Replace placeholder text with your exact pricing (e.g., "from £89/month")
3. Upload a real before/after photo or Dr Gaurav's photo into the image frame
4. Update practice logo and contact details
5. Download as 1080×1080px JPG (Facebook feed) or 1080×1920px PNG (Story)

### Step 2 — Launch in Meta Ads Manager
- Use the exact ad copy from `08-AD-COPY-BANK.md` — all headlines are 30 chars or under (Google-ready)
- Target: 25–55, radius 10 miles from practice, interest: cosmetic dentistry, teeth whitening
- Budget: £30/day per practice in week 1 (see `08-AD-COPY-BANK.md` for full £800 week-1 budget split)

### Step 3 — A/B test
- Run recommended design vs Variant B for 7 days minimum
- Winning design (lower CPL) gets full budget in week 2
- Kill the loser. Never run 4 ads simultaneously to the same audience — it kills reach

### Step 4 — Report back
- WhatsApp us the week-1 results (impressions, clicks, enquiries, CPL)
- We will optimise targeting and creative based on actual data
