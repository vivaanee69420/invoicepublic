## PART 3: THE 5 PROVEN AD CREATIVE FORMULAS

Reverse-engineered from the above confirmed performers:

### Formula 1: The Price Reveal (Banning's model)
```
[HOOK]: "[CITY], are you embarrassed by your teeth? 😬"
[REVEAL]: "Invisalign from just £X/month at [Practice Name]"  
[CREDENTIAL]: "Top X% of Invisalign providers in Europe"
[OFFER]: "Free consultation — limited spaces this month"
[CTA]: "Book now — link in bio"
```
**When to use:** High-volume acquisition; requires Diamond+ tier to make economics work.

### Formula 2: The Free Preview (Parrock/Aligndent model)
```
[HOOK]: "Want to see your new smile BEFORE you start treatment?"
[VALUE]: "Free digital iTero 3D scan — see your results before you commit"
[CREDENTIAL]: "Diamond Apex Invisalign Provider — Europe's top 1%"
[PROOF]: "X patients have already transformed their smile"
[CTA]: "Book your free smile preview — [town], [date]"
```
**When to use:** Mid-to-premium positioning; reduces risk perception; strong for confident closers.

### Formula 3: The Daily Cost Frame (Parrock's implant model)
```
[HOOK]: "Missing teeth? It could be fixed for less than £X a day"
[VALUE]: "Full arch dental implants — same day treatment available"
[OFFER]: "Free consultation + free CT scan (worth £250) — [month] only"
[PROOF]: "X implants placed over Y years"
[CTA]: "Claim your free scan — limited appointments"
```
**When to use:** High-value treatment (implants, All-on-4); eliminates sticker shock by anchoring to daily cost.

### Formula 4: The Transformation Story (EvoDental/Smiles By Gurms model)
```
[HOOK]: "[Patient name] couldn't smile for [X] years. Here's what happened in ONE day..."
[STORY]: 15–30 second patient video — before footage, treatment reaction, result
[SOCIAL PROOF]: "X five-star reviews"
[CTA]: "See if you qualify — free consultation"
```
**When to use:** Highest converting format for implants and significant cosmetic cases; requires signed patient consent.

### Formula 5: The Seasonal Drop (Ruh's model)
```
[HOOK]: "Our LOWEST [treatment] prices EVER — [date] only 🔥"
[OFFER]: "[Treatment] from £X — ends [date]"
[GUARANTEE]: "Not happy? 100% money back" (if you can stand behind this)
[FINANCE]: "From just £X/month, 0% finance"
[CTA]: "Book before [date] — link in bio"
```
**When to use:** Periodic (quarterly) to drive lead spikes; do not use so often it loses urgency.
