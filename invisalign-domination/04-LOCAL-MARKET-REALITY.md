## 1. What £999 Invisalign Actually Is — The Full Economic Picture

You asked the right question. Let's answer it properly.

### The hard floor: Align Technology lab fees

Align Technology does not publish UK lab fees. From multiple independent sources (US-origin, applicable directionally):

- Full Comprehensive case lab fee at **no-tier discount**: ~£1,500 per case
- With retainers added: rises to ~£1,800–£2,000
- Lab fees represent approximately **40% of the patient-facing price** for a typical case

**At zero discount, a £999 price loses money on the lab fee alone.** There is no version of this that works unless:
(a) the product is NOT genuine Invisalign, or
(b) the provider is at Diamond Plus tier (up to 50% discount → lab cost ~£750–800), or
(c) £999 is not the all-in price

### What actually gets advertised at £999–£1,500

| What's advertised | What it actually is |
|---|---|
| "Invisalign from £999" | Own-brand or third-party aligner (ClearCorrect, Smile White, SureSmile) — NOT Invisalign |
| DTC brands: Caspersmile from £625, ALIGNERCO ~£747, NewSmile £1,295 | No dentist involvement; GDC grey area; SmileDirectClub model — and SDC is now bankrupt |
| mydentist ClearCorrect from £1,490 | NOT Invisalign; ClearCorrect is a competitor aligner system |
| UrgentCare Dental "from £999" | Own-brand aligners |
| Smile White from £995 via dentist network | NOT Invisalign |
| Impress (smile2impress.com) from £1,850 | **IS genuine Invisalign** — chain clinics using volume discount; Impress is an Invisalign provider, not an alternative |

**No Kent or North London practice was found advertising genuine Invisalign at £999.** The lowest confirmed genuine Invisalign prices found in your catchments:
- Angel Smile, Islington: from £1,220 (simple/per arch case, Platinum Provider)
- Providence Dental, Folkestone: from £1,500
- Emergency Dentist Maidstone: from £1,800
- Banning Dental, Wembley: from £1,995 all-inclusive (Diamond Plus — this is the real version of the cheap model; they have the 50% discount to make it work)

### The economics of a genuine £2,000 Invisalign Lite case at Diamond Apex

| Item | Cost |
|---|---|
| Lite lab fee pre-discount (~£650) × 54% (46% off) | £351 |
| Retainers (2 arches Vivera) | £200 |
| Consumables, attachments | £75 |
| **Total COGS** | **£626** |
| Associate 45% split on (£2,000 − £351) | £742 |
| CPA (blended) | £150–200 |
| Fixed overhead per case | £150–200 |
| **Net contribution** | **~£50–282 (2–14%)** |

This only works at Apex volume (otherwise lab fee is higher), with near-zero paid CPA (organic only), and for the simplest Lite cases. It does NOT work for a practice below Platinum tier. It is barely viable even at Diamond Apex.

### Is the cheap end a threat to you?

**Direct threat: NO.** Patients buying £999 aligners are NOT your patients. Price-motivated buyers will choose the cheapest regardless of quality. You are not competing for them.

**Indirect threat: YES, but specific.** The risk is:
1. **Impress** (£1,850–£3,890, genuine Invisalign via chain clinic) is the real mid-market competitor — they are expanding UK locations and have a credible model.
2. The existence of sub-£2,000 prices anchors patient expectations and requires you to justify your £4,000+ fee clearly.
3. **CMA market investigation (March 2026)** is specifically looking at hidden fees and bait-and-switch dental pricing. If you advertise prominently, be scrupulously transparent.

**Your counter:** Diamond/Apex badge + before/afters + TCO experience + GDC-compliant clinician-led care + named clinician brand. The DTC/cheap-brand model (SmileDirectClub) already collapsed. The GDC is tightening. Your compliance is your advantage.
