The five levers in priority order:
1. Climb to Diamond/Apex (COGS drops £250–400/case)

# BUSINESS 2: LAB
## The Dental Laboratory

### What I Know
The lab is a separate revenue and operations arm. In the context of the £100M ecosystem, the lab serves multiple functions:
1. **Internal**: processes lab work for the Practice and Fixed Solutions cases
2. **External**: potentially serves other dental practices — a B2B revenue stream
3. **Strategic**: in-house lab capability = faster turnaround, quality control, and critically, **margin protection** (lab COGS is the single biggest variable cost in dental practice)

The Invisalign strategy identified that the biggest margin leak in dental is lab cost and associate splits. If your Lab is internal, you recapture margin that currently flows out to external labs.

# BUSINESS 3: ACADEMY
## The Training and Education Arm

### What the Market Data Tells Me

This is the arm with the highest margin, highest reach, and highest strategic value of the four — and it is massively underbuilt across the UK market.

**The benchmark:** Sandeep Kumar's *Mastering Your Invisalign Business* charges £895 for a 1-day course, claims 1,500+ dentists trained. At £895 × 1,500 dentists = **£1.34M from one product alone**. And that is the starter product.

**The real opportunity you have that Sandeep does not:**
- You have a Lab (he doesn't — or it isn't central to his model)
- You have Fixed Solutions capability (not his focus)
- You operate across two distinct markets (Kent + London)
- You are building this in 2026 — the market is bigger than when he started

**The Academy ecosystem (what it can become):**
1. **Entry product**: 1-day Invisalign business masterclass (like Sandeep's — £895–£1,500/head, 15–20 delegates)
2. **Mid-tier**: 3–6 month mentorship programme (clinical + business, £5,000–£12,000)
3. **High-ticket**: Done-with-you group practice accelerator (monthly retainer or equity model)
4. **Lab partnership**: Academy delegates send their lab work to your Lab (B2B revenue that compounds from the academy)
5. **Community/membership**: Annual recurring revenue from alumni community
6. **Digital products**: Courses, templates, systems sold online (scales without chairs)

**The competitors offering coaching (and their weaknesses):**

- Aligner Confidence: £12,000 entry — premium but high barrier
- Most others: clinical-only (no business curriculum)
- **Your gap**: a complete programme that covers clinical excellence AND business scaling AND lab integration AND fixed-to-aligner conversion pathways

# BUSINESS 4: FIXED SOLUTIONS
## Fixed Orthodontics, Implants, Bridges, Restorative

### What I Know
Fixed Solutions is the highest average case value arm of the four businesses. Where Invisalign cases run £2,500–£5,500, implant and complex fixed cases regularly reach £5,000–£30,000+ per patient.

**Why this arm matters for the £100M:**
- Fixed cases create the highest single-patient revenue events
- Patients who do Invisalign often want cosmetic and restorative work afterwards — the Practice feeds Fixed Solutions naturally
- Fixed Solutions patients who need ongoing maintenance return to the Practice
- The Lab supports Fixed Solutions with crowns, bridges, and implant components

**The integration opportunity nobody is fully exploiting:**
A patient who comes in for Invisalign, completes treatment, then needs a crown on a damaged tooth, implants for missing teeth, and veneers for the final smile — if all of that happens within your ecosystem, that is a £15,000–£40,000 patient lifetime value vs a £3,500 Invisalign case.

The "Smile Journey" upsell from Invisalign → whitening → veneers → implants → maintenance is the highest-value patient pathway in modern dentistry.
