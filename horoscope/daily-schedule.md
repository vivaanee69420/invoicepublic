## THREE RULES THAT OVERRIDE EVERYTHING

**Rule 1:** The 4 AM block is never negotiated away. If you have an early flight, you wake at 3 AM. If you had a late night, you still wake at 4 AM (and sleep earlier the following night). The 4 AM block is the foundation. Without it, the rest of the day is reactive.

**Rule 2:** Thursday mornings are sacred. No clinical work. No admin. No meetings that aren't about expansion. If someone books a Thursday morning appointment, your team declines it.

**Rule 3:** Every Saturday morning, before anything else, you pull 10 numbers that tell you the health of your business. Revenue. Cases. Acceptance rate. Pipeline. Deals. If you don't know these numbers by heart on Saturday morning, you are managing by feeling, not by data.
