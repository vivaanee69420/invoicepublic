## HOW TO MAKE THE FIRST CONTACT
### Template (adapt for each person)

**LinkedIn DM:**
> Hi [Name], I've been following your work at [Company] — the [specific thing they did] is exactly the direction I think UK dental is heading.
>
> I'm building a premium Invisalign-focused group in [Kent/North London], currently at [X locations], targeting a significant expansion over the next 3 years.
>
> Would you be open to a 20-minute call? I'm genuinely interested in your perspective on [specific relevant question].

**What makes this work:**
- Specific reference (shows you did homework)
- Clear statement of what you're building (no vagueness)
- A question about them (not a pitch)
- 20 minutes (easy yes, specific ask)
