## THE DAILY 5-MINUTE LEADERSHIP CALL
*Gaurav does this every day at 5:00 PM — 4 calls, 5 minutes each, one per business line*

**The exact script (same every day):**

> "Give me your three numbers:
> 1. What was expected today?
> 2. What actually happened?
> 3. What do you need from me to fix or improve it?"

That is all. 5 minutes. If they don't have the numbers, that is itself important information.
